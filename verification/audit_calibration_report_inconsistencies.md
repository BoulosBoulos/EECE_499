## Findings

### F1 (B) — §5c "Fusion-aux at-or-below DRPPO on 1a — mechanism analysis" is outdated by the post-Step-12 dual-critic bug fix
- **Report claim**: §5c interprets Fusion-aux's mean SR 0.948 (Δ = −0.012 vs DRPPO 0.960 on 1a) as an "architectural conflict" between the optimality (Soft-HJB) and safety (CBF) auxiliary critics that are *jointly distilled into the actor*.
- **Current reality**: post-`fusion_pre_tier1_verification.json` (verdict: BUG) and `fusion_fix_verification.json`, the pre-fix Fusion-aux had `U_safety` trained but **never queried by the policy** (effective `w_distill_cbf = 0`). The "joint distillation" the report describes was not actually happening — it was Soft-HJB-aux distillation only, with a parallel-trained but unused U_safety.
- **Fix applied**: `models/pde/fusion_aux_agent.py` now performs a convex combination of `U_optimality` and `U_safety` for both the value-head distillation and the actor-KL soft-policy q-values (per the artifact in `verification/fusion_bug_artifact.md`).
- **Impact on report**: §5c's mechanism analysis (lines 86-88) is now historically inaccurate. The pre-fix Fusion-aux was effectively `Soft-HJB-aux + parallel-trained-but-unused U_safety`. The "architectural conflict" framing applies only to the post-fix architecture; the calibration data in §4 was generated from the pre-fix code.
- **Recommended action**: append a §5c-addendum or §6.5 noting the bug discovery + fix:
  > "**Post-publication addendum (commit 4c5ead7)**: the Step 11 Fusion-aux runs analyzed in §5c were generated by a pre-fix implementation in which `U_safety` was trained on the CBF residual but never queried by the policy distillation. The 'architectural conflict' framing of this section reflects the structural relationship between optimality-distillation and the (then-silenced) safety critic. The fix in `models/pde/fusion_aux_agent.py` (verification/fusion_bug_artifact.md) restores the dual-critic policy influence via a convex combination weighted by `w_optimality / w_safety`. Fusion-aux's behavior on Tier 1 will reflect the post-fix architecture and may differ from the §4 numbers above."
- **Severity**: B. Affects how the paper frames Fusion-aux's contribution. The mechanism narrative needs to be split into pre-fix (Step 11) and post-fix (Tier 1) regimes.
